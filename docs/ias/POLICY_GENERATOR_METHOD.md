# Privacy Policy and Terms Drafting Method

**Document:** AQ-IAS-07 · **Prepared:** 7 November 2026

## Recommended generator

The professor-recommended [App Privacy Policy Generator by Nishant Srivastava (nisrulz)](https://github.com/nisrulz/app-privacy-policy-generator) was reviewed at revision **906e635798b3bf925c67d90558a25858d544b93f**. The source templates were used as a coverage guide to revise the project-specific drafts. This was a source-template-assisted drafting review, not a completed hosted wizard export or legal approval. No business/customer data was submitted to the generator, and its software is not included in the app.

Reviewed sources:

- [Privacy template](https://github.com/nisrulz/app-privacy-policy-generator/blob/906e635798b3bf925c67d90558a25858d544b93f/src/tpl/privacy-simple.html).
- [Terms template](https://github.com/nisrulz/app-privacy-policy-generator/blob/906e635798b3bf925c67d90558a25858d544b93f/src/tpl/tnc-simple.html).
- [English content](https://github.com/nisrulz/app-privacy-policy-generator/blob/906e635798b3bf925c67d90558a25858d544b93f/src/locales/en.json).

The project drafts use original, app-specific wording rather than copying the generator's source or representing its generic clauses as verified app behavior.

## Adaptation record

| Generator topic | Project-specific treatment | Draft location |
|---|---|---|
| Information collection and use | Describe actual customer, account, order, expense and security records, rather than automatically claiming browsing analytics. | Privacy §§2–3 |
| Providers and transfers | Identify Supabase; require confirmation of region and provider arrangements. | Privacy §4; Terms §10 |
| AI disclosure | Explain advisory local expense classification, Owner confirmation and private reviewed training data. | Privacy §5; Terms §6 |
| Rights, retention and deletion | Apply RA 10173 §§11, 12 and 16 and covered accounting-retention rules. Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud commercial records. | Privacy §§3, 6–7 |
| Security and breach response | Describe safeguards and conditional NPC notification duties without promising perfect security. | Privacy §8 |
| Changes and contact | Retain honest placeholders for the operator/contact/effective date; provide versioned notice requirements. | Privacy §§1, 9–10; Terms §§8–10 |
| Internet access and updates | Explain that financial writes need a backend and updates require operator communication; retain truthful unknown-save behavior. | Terms §§4, 7, 10 |
| Service suspension and acceptable use | Preserve assigned Owner/Staff roles and existing revocation rules. Do not import public-social-network or paid-subscription clauses. | Terms §§1–2, 5, 8 |
| Foreign-law and generic commercial clauses | Do not assume GDPR/CCPA applicability, public registration, advertisements, marketing, automatic all-data erasure, or an app license the operator has not selected. | Both drafts remain scoped to the approved Philippine station application. |

## Deliverables and limits

The [Privacy Policy](DRAFT_PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and [Terms](DRAFT_TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md) remain drafts. The operator's actual identity, contact, lawful-purpose decisions, retention schedule, provider arrangements, effective date and acceptance process require completion before organizational adoption. General project authorization does not invent those facts. These drafts support the academic submission; software tests and generator assistance do not establish legal certification.
